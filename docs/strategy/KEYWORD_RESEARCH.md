# RankPilot AI — Search Intent, Entity & Keyword Strategy

**Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Scope:** Search Intent Mapping, Semantic Entity Architecture, AEO/GEO Clusters, and Anti-Cannibalization Plan  

---

## 1. Strategy & Philosophy

1. **Search Intent Over Keyword Density:** We reject arbitrary keyword density. Search engines and AI answer engines index concepts, entities, and solutions. Each page targets a distinct primary search intent.
2. **Anti-Cannibalization Architecture:** We enforce strict page-to-intent boundaries. The homepage targets the overarching platform intent; subpages target modular capabilities and audience workflows.
3. **Entity-First Grounding:** We ground RankPilot AI in recognized Knowledge Graph entities: `SoftwareApplication`, `SearchEngineOptimization`, `GoogleSearchConsole`, `WebsiteAuditing`, `SchemaOrg`.

## 4. Entity & Semantic Architecture Rules

1. **Unambiguous Brand Identity:** RankPilot AI is always explicitly described with its complete category phrase: *"an AI SEO intelligence and action management platform built for SEO professionals"*.
2. **Contextual Natural Anchor Text:** When linking from feature cards or the homepage to subpages, anchors describe the precise capability (e.g., *"explore striking-distance keyword discovery"*, *"review automated re-crawl verification"*), avoiding generic *"click here"* or repetitive exact-match phrases.
3. **Structured Q&A for Answer Engines (AEO):** Every major section begins with an editorial question or direct statement followed immediately by a 40–60 word factual answer, allowing search bots to extract featured snippets and conversational LLMs to cite RankPilot AI directly.
