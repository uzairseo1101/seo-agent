## 1. Conversion Funnel Philosophy: Value-First, Zero-Hype

SEO professionals are analytical buyers who cannot be pressured with fake countdown timers or fabricated scarcity. 

RankPilot AI's conversion strategy is built on **Radical Demonstration & Immediate Value Delivery**:
1. **Show, Don't Tell:** Instead of describing what the agent does in vague buzzwords, we provide interactive, high-fidelity demonstrations of the actual software.
2. **Remove Risk at Every Touchpoint:**
   - No credit card required.
   - Read-only safe Google Search Console integration (zero risk of modifying client properties).
   - Cancel anytime with one click.
3. **Transparent Operational Capabilities:** Clearly explain crawler limits, data sources, and scoring mathematics upfront so agencies trust the tool from day one.

## 3. CTA Placement & Visual Hierarchy Rules

### 3.1 Strict 1-Primary-Button Rule
On every viewport, there is **strictly one green primary CTA button** (`#059669`). All other buttons are secondary white cards with 1px border or ghost buttons.

### 3.2 CTA Placements Across the Page
1. **Sticky Header:**
   - Secondary: `Sign in` (Ghost button)
   - Primary: `Start free` (Green filled button, 40px height)
2. **Hero Section:**
   - Primary: `Start free` (Green button, 40px height, right arrow)
   - Secondary: `Explore the platform` (White bordered button, scrolls to showcase)
   - Reassurance: *Read-only safe Google Search Console integration · No credit card required*
3. **How It Works Walkthrough:**
   - Link: *See the full walkthrough $\rightarrow$*
4. **Interactive Showcase:**
   - Contextual: *Launch Agency Plan* / *Start Free Solo Pilot*
5. **Pricing Section:**
   - Single primary CTA placed on the recommended **Agency Plan** (*Start Agency Plan*).
   - Freelancer and Team cards use secondary bordered buttons to maintain visual focus.
6. **Final CTA Band:**
   - Large prominent primary button: *Get Started with RankPilot AI*.

## 5. Ethical Conversion Standards (Zero-Hype Policy)

1. **No Artificial Urgency:** We do not display fake countdown timers, fake seat counters, or "limited time only" discount popups.
2. **No Fabricated Social Proof:** Customer logos, user counts, and testimonials are only displayed when authorized by actual pilot customers. Until then, product capability proof and transparent methodology take center stage.
3. **No Unprovable Guarantees:** We never promise "guaranteed #1 rankings" or "100% traffic surges". We promise accurate diagnostics, prioritized work, and verified fixes.
